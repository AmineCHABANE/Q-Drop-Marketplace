Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 7, 2026

Q-Drop Marketplace ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or make a purchase.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum data necessary to process your order and deliver access to the Products:

  • Payment data: Processed entirely by Stripe or PayPal. We do not store your credit card or financial information. Only a transaction reference may be retained.
  • Email address: Provided by you when requesting GitHub access. Used solely to deliver your purchase and respond to support queries.
  • GitHub username: Provided by you post-purchase. Used to grant repository access.
  • Usage data: Standard server logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited) may be collected automatically by our hosting provider (GitHub Pages). This data is anonymized and used for security and analytics.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Process and fulfill your purchase
  • Grant access to purchased GitHub repositories
  • Respond to customer support requests
  • Send transaction confirmations and important account updates
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not use your data for marketing without your explicit consent, and we do not sell your personal data to third parties.

3. Third-Party Processors

We use the following third-party services to operate the marketplace:

  • Stripe: Payment processing. Stripe's Privacy Policy applies to data collected during checkout: stripe.com/privacy
  • PayPal: Alternative payment processing. PayPal's Privacy Policy applies: paypal.com/privacy
  • GitHub: Repository hosting and access delivery. GitHub's Privacy Statement applies to repository usage: github.com/privacy

4. Data Retention

We retain transaction references and your email address for up to 3 years for legal and accounting purposes, then securely delete this data. GitHub access is maintained as long as the repository exists. You may request deletion of your personal data at any time (see Section 7).

5. Cookies

Our website (hosted on GitHub Pages) does not set cookies directly. Third-party payment processors (Stripe, PayPal) may set cookies during the checkout process. These are governed by their respective privacy policies.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. Payment data is handled exclusively by PCI-compliant processors (Stripe, PayPal) and never stored on our servers. Email communication is transmitted over TLS-encrypted connections.

7. Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Erasure: Request deletion of your personal data
  • Portability: Request your data in a machine-readable format
  • Objection: Object to certain processing of your data

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at Aminechabane7@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, contact us immediately.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

10. Contact

For any privacy-related questions or requests, contact us at: Aminechabane7@gmail.com
